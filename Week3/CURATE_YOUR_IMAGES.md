## 4. Ruthless Rejection Notes (AI Discernment Audit)

The core skill of AI Fluency is **knowing what to reject**. Below are the concrete rejections made during this iteration:

### ❌ Rejection 1: The "Glowing Holographic Brain" (AI Slop)
* **Prompt Tested**: `"Futuristic glowing 3D neural brain floating above computer screen with neon circuit lines, dark background, 8k"`
* **Why It Was Rejected**:
  > *"Classic 'AI slop'. It carries zero information about front-end architecture, consumes valuable viewport space, and makes the portfolio look like an uncurated crypto landing page. It upstages the work instead of framing it."*

---

### ❌ Rejection 2: The "Empathetic Humanoid Robot" (Uncanny Valley & Ethical Violation)
* **Prompt Tested**: `"Photorealistic AI android robot gently comforting a distressed human in a clinical setting, soft lighting"`
* **Why It Was Rejected**:
  > *"Creepy uncanny valley aesthetic. More importantly, it violates mental health software ethics by visually suggesting an AI robot replaces licensed human psychiatric care. In a healthcare triage tool, framing must remain grounded and responsible."*

---

### ❌ Rejection 3: The "Iridescent Melting Glass Balls" (Senseless Dribbble Trend)
* **Prompt Tested**: `"Floating translucent iridescent glass spheres with colorful liquid refraction, dark glassmorphic studio"`
* **Why It Was Rejected**:
  > *"Iridescent melting spheres are visually busy and completely disconnected from the problem space. They clash with the calm `#090D16` palette and draw the eye away from the TTFT latency benchmarks."*
