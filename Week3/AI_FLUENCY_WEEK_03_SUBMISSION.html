Two-Line Style Note:

Typography uses clean Inter paired with JetBrains Mono; palette is a calm deep navy (#090D16) with crisp near-white text (#F1F5F9) and disciplined indigo (#6366F1) / emergency rose (#F43F5E) accents. The aesthetic is an engineering-grade dark cockpit: high-contrast, WCAG AA compliant, and quiet enough that live code proof remains the brightest element on screen.

3. Curate Images & AI Rejection Audit

AI was tested to generate image concepts. All 3 AI image proposals were rejected on purpose in favor of real, unedited code and UI captures:

❌ REJECTED Concept 1: "Glowing 3D holographic neural brain with neon circuits"
Reason: Classic "AI slop". Adds zero proof of front-end engineering, clutters viewport, and looks like a generic crypto website.

❌ REJECTED Concept 2: "Photorealistic AI humanoid robot counseling a patient"
Reason: Uncanny valley effect. Violates mental health design ethics by falsely implying AI replaces licensed clinical psychiatrists.

❌ REJECTED Concept 3: "Melting iridescent glass spheres floating in dark space"
Reason: Overused generic Dribbble trend. Zero semantic connection to latency benchmarks, accessibility, or Hick's law triage.

✅ THE WINNER: Real, unedited high-DPI screenshots of the actual build.
Reason: In technical portfolio engineering, REAL UI PROOF always beats AI-generated illustrations.
